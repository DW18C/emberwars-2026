PALMON SURVIVAL — EMBER WARS

📋 Overview

🗓️4-week seasonal PvP event starting week 4 of Season 1
⚔️Factions: Frost Legion vs Ember Alliance (reassigned weekly)
🏰Top 16 guilds per server (by embers) join each faction — 32 guilds per faction
🔁Two rounds per week: each faction attacks once

Qualification Requirements

  • Guild holds ≥ 20,000 embers
  • Guild has built a guild generator
  • Guild ranks top 16 by embers on its server

🏆 Why Help Other Guilds? (Reward Structure)

There are two separate reward tracks at the end of the event:

  1. Individual performance — your own personal contribution
  2. Server-wide ember total — how many embers the whole server accumulated collectively

Goal: finish #1 in both categories. Helping neighboring guilds cancel enemy comets protects the server's collective ember pool — directly supporting reward track #2.

💰 How to Get Embers

  • Capture and hold map structures
  • Occupy coal mines (levels 1–6) and shrines/sanctums — boosts generator upgrades too
  • Win PvP events (Ember Wars, Server Showdowns)
  • Burn and loot enemy generators during the battle stage

🧊 Camp Temperature Management

  • Each dispatched squad reduces camp temperature by -3°C
  • Below -20°C for too long = camp freezes (no rallies/teleports)
  • Rush Brick Kilns to level 15 for generator Overdrive fuel
  • Receiving 1,000 coal from guildmates raises temperature by 4°C

⚔️ ATTACK DAY — Step by Step

1

Pre-War Prep

  • Switch to Fighter class (Class Reset Scroll) (optional — recommended once comfortable, not required to join)
  • Skill into Cheerleader (attack buff) + Healing Fruits (optional — recommended once comfortable, not required to join)
  • Max out troop training before the battle window opens
  • Heal your hospital fully
2

Launching the Comet (Main Attack Method)

  1. Assign one player to carry the Paragon and relocate them roughly 180–200km from the target generator
  2. Surround the Paragon carrier with as many guild members as possible
  3. Pop your own 8-hour shields ("bubbles") while protecting the Paragon — being shielded means you can focus on protecting without worrying about being attacked
  4. Only send your FIRST team to protect the carrier. If that team is defeated, send it back again immediately
  5. Activate the comet — it takes 6 minutes to charge
  6. The Paragon carrier cannot be shielded — the moment they activate the comet, enemies will rush to attack them to try to stop the launch, so the protectors must intercept anyone attacking the carrier at that exact moment
Tip — Ophidian Ability: With 1 minute left before the comet falls, activate the Ophidian ability and give yourself 2–3 health bars. This helps you survive teleporting next to the enemy generator right after the comet lands.
3

When the Comet Lands

  • The comet teleports all enemy bases away from the target area
  • Immediately teleport your strongest squads onto the freed tiles around the enemy generator
  • Attack the generator with all squadrons until destroyed
  • 100 successful attacks are required to destroy the generator
  • If executed correctly, victory typically comes 2–3 minutes after the comet falls
Discipline Rule: Ignore anyone who teleports next to you (unless they're especially disruptive). Stay focused exclusively on attacking the rival generator — don't get pulled into side-fights.
4

Alternate (Slower) Method

Attack the generator directly from nearby positions if you can physically reach them without a comet. Works, but takes much longer than the Comet Strike method.

5

After Winning

Move as quickly as possible to help neighboring guilds still fighting. Redeploy your guild to assist other servers/guilds under attack (e.g., once your own target is defeated, move on to help another guild's fight).

🛡️ DEFENSE DAY — Step by Step

Defense Day isn't just about holding your own generator — it's an active, roaming counter-operation to protect your server's neighboring guilds too.

1

Disable the Enemy Paragon Carrier (Targeting Your Generator)

  • Smaller camps and players who can't actively participate occupy every teleport tile surrounding your generator (the "Hive" strategy) — this keeps gaps from opening up near the generator
  • Shield these tile-holders so enemy camps can't attack and teleport them away, which would leave a tile open right next to the generator
  • Place Cheerleader buff directly under the generator, if your guild has one available
  • With the Hive held and shielded, stronger players are freed up to leave and actively hunt the enemy Paragon carrier
  • Identify the enemy player carrying the Paragon aimed at your generator
  • Attack that player directly — reduce the durability bar of their base — this cancels their comet launch entirely before it finishes charging (fast individual marches, not slow rallies)
2

Defend Against a Direct March (No Comet)

Canceling the enemy's comet stops that specific launch, but it doesn't make your generator safe — they can still follow up with a regular direct march/attack (the "Alternate Method"). Stay and hold your ground in case this happens:

  • Re-teleport onto cleared tiles immediately
  • Dismiss non-combat squads
  • Split roles between garrison-refill and counter-attack
3

Help Other Guilds (Roaming Support)

Only move on to this once you're confident your own generator is safe.

  1. Identify the enemy player carrying the Paragon for a comet attack on an allied/neighboring guild
  2. Attack that player directly — reduce the durability bar of their base — this cancels their comet launch
  3. Move on to help the next guild under threat
